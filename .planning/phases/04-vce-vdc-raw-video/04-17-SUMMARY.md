# Phase 4 Plan 17: Guest interrupt measurements and the toy machinery Summary

**A spin-counter fixture takes RCR and VBlank interrupts through a table-driven handler chain for four frames at 262 and 263 lines, a kind compares the guest's iteration counts with the dev log read stamps and the hand-derived 245, 341 and 1,610 master clock gaps of the three model variants, and the toy machinery gained a pass column, a judge range and two toys, one of which turns the offset group red while the assumption-free ratio stays green.**

## What was built

- **Task 1, tracer (commit 33f206a).** `spin.inc` (the loop `inc a`, `bra` of 6 cycles and 18 master clocks from the H8-25 table, the cost rule, ADD24) and `vdc_irq.s`: handlers `h_gen`, `h_last`, `h_end`, `h_sync`, `h_fin`, `h_noack`, 95 events per cycle (RCR 64 to 97 every third line, 100 to 108 on consecutive lines, 110, 113 to 299 every third line, 301, 303, the VBlank event, 306 to 324), the sample and status logs in work RAM (one 256-byte page per cycle), and an analysis in the foreground after the chain ends (24-bit delta sums with clc/adc). The 16 checks: ratio of RCR 100 to 108 against 8 times 100 to 101 in two cycles (GUEST), the iterations in 100 lines and in four frames against 1365-clock literals (ASSUMED LED-VID-0001), flag order and the two-line bound, all statuses of four cycles, bits 6 and 7, the cycle counter, the no-acknowledge re-entry count (five entries, ASSUMED LED-IRQ-0009), and four raw observe rows. `vdc_irq_agree` in `videokinds.c` runs the ROM under the three default power-on policies (whole DSV1 block equal), reads the status read stamps of the first run from the dev log and judges the gap and hit windows. Cases for 262 and 263 lines (the 263 ones bind `bind.lines_per_frame=263`), two sweeps, four agreement cases, A10 of 04-VALIDATION.md.
- **Task 2 (commit 6f95305).** `conformance-run toy-expect` and `toy-verdict`, `controls_mode` through them, the selftest extension; `judge=A-B` and `judge_dsv1_range`; the case `vdc_irq_ratio`; the toys `vid-rcr-base` and `status-read-keeps-flags`; the four ledger pins with their case sides.
- **Fix (commit b0da731).** The ratio tolerance went back to the plan's 9 (the first version used the worst-case bound 12); both manifests repinned.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] A sync handler (h_sync).** The plan does not mention it. The vertical registers are written about 28 lines into frame 0, the first display window starts at line 45 and is cut by the frame wrap at line 262, so a chain started at once is out of step with the table; h_sync consumes the first RCR = 64 match and the chain starts at the next one. Cost: the run is about five frames long (the guest is done at about 1.98 million master clocks, until_mclk is 2,100,000; the insn sink of opcode_exercise stays below its cap, about 217,000 of 262,144 records).
2. **[Note] The vector trampoline is counted.** The plan's cost formula (entry 8 + body + RTI 7) leaves out the `jmp (VEC_IRQ1)` of startup.inc, which every handler pays (7 cycles). It is in every sum and in the 72-cycle budget (`.assert` at link time); h_last is 66 cycles, so the budget held with 6 cycles to spare.
3. **[Note] 16 checks, not about 26.** 12 judged rows and 4 observe rows. The plan's "about 26" was an estimate; every row of the behaviour list is covered (ratio, line and frame period, flag order and the two-line bound, statuses and level semantics with the no-acknowledge count, the gap and hit windows through the agreement kind).
4. **[Note] The hit row is the VD to RCR = 64 interval of the next frame** (two observe rows), which carries both the 263-line frame length and the rcr_origin shift, instead of a separate RCR = 64 + k sample; the 1,610 window is the same fact as the one-line shift (245 + 1365).
5. **[Note] Tolerance 9 and brk 12 are derived or taken from the plan, not tuned.** tol = 27 master clocks (1.5 iterations) is the plan window; brk = 12 (under 4 cycles) is the bound that the interrupt wait gives, and 12 + 12 = 24 is under 27. The ratio uses the plan's 9 (see key decisions); the first version used 12 and was changed in its own commit.
6. **[Note] The guest cases mark the raw rows observe=IDX:policy** (the grammar has no item that keeps equality without a value), so their equality across policies is waived there; the agreement kind compares the whole DSV1 block under the three policies instead.
7. **[Note] The toy EXPECT lists more ids than the plan:** vdc_irq_263, vdc_irq_agree_hdw and vdc_irq_agree_lb were observed red under vid-rcr-base and are listed ("any other id observed red"). vdc_irq was observed red (guest_failure), so the stop condition did not apply.
8. **[Note] Files beyond the plan list:** `fixtures/manifest/*.toml` are listed; `tests/conformance/cases/vdc_stat.toml` (the two pins), `tests/conformance/REQUIRED` (nine ids) and `scripts/check` (selftest) are in the plan. `toy_expect` and `toy_verdict` are new modes of `conformance-run` instead of inline edits at the four sites, so the selftest can drive them.
9. **[Note] Plan estimate was 85,000 tokens; the realized diff is about 22,300** by chars/4 over the added lines (32 files, 1,337 insertions, 24 deletions). The stop rule did not fire.
10. **[Note] The standard plan-complete step may tick VID-04** in REQUIREMENTS.md (the plan lists it; 04-16 lists all six ids and completes them); the tick is reverted when it appears, as after 04-08, so the checkbox stays open until 04-16.
