**Detail 1.** `src/core/vid.c` `ds_vid_render` writes a 64-byte DSVR v1 header with these fields: origin u64, frame, line, frame_lines, line_mclk, div, vce_cr, active_x, active_w, border, and the latched BXR/BYR, HSR, HDR, VPR, VDW, VCR, MWR and CR. After the header come u16 index[active_w] and u16 colour[active_w], the colour being the 9-bit palette value.
The renderer reads a const instance and owns no line buffer.
The fixtures are `bg_basic`, `bg_enab` and `bg_scroll`. Each `.s` file has hand-packed tiles and a BAT. In each `.vexp` file the literals are derived in comments from the fixture's own BAT, tile and palette formula. No literal comes from a run or a hash.
`video_bg_scroll` passes 4/4: struct ASSUMED, latch MAN H7-7 (rows `bxr1+1`, `bxr2+1`), latch_rel MAN, byr_next MAN H7-7 (`byr1+1` shows BAT row 8, i.e. BYR+1), byr_own and byr_rel ASSUMED. I re-read the H7-7 page image from a local scratch copy. The page says BXR "will become valid with the next scanning line" and BYR does the same "(BYR+1)", which matches LED-VID-0009.
`video_bg_basic_solid` holds solid MAN H7-18 and solidval/border GUEST. `video_bg_enab` holds bb_on and toggle_own MAN H7-6. `video_bg_scroll_asym` (the 64x64 wrap) is ASSUMED LED-VID-0010.
The frame line count is a literal: 262 in vce_pal/vce_dot, and 263 in `vid_frame_lines` under `bind.lines_per_frame=263`. The origins are checked by the `step=1365` STRUCT rows. Chunk and restore invariance on a scroll scene: `chunk_equiv_video_bg_scroll`, `snapshot_restore_video_bg` pass.

**Detail 2.** `vdc_reg` has 37 checks. The MAN H7-3/5/7/25 rows cover the VRR read-ahead, AR masking and the IW steps. The GUEST vram-sum rows check TIA uploads. `vdc_vram`, `vdc_vram_sweep` and `vdc_regmask` pass.
`vdc_dma` (24 checks) verifies the moved words with GUEST sums of the destination and with MAN H7-11/H7-12 rows: LENR+1 words, the SI/D and DI/D first/last words, DV set and read-clear (H7-4).
`vdc_satb_data` compares all 256 SATB words with the pattern the guest wrote, under three power-on policies (768 assertions). It is a host-side kind because SATB is not guest-readable.
`vdc_stat` (21 checks) reads the status in guest handlers. Rows 5, 6, 9, 10, 17, 18 and 21 are MAN H7-4 rows for VD, RR, DV and DS set and clear-on-read.
Level tags: LED-VID-0006/0007/0008 and LED-IRQ-0009 start with VL0, LED-VID-0013/0014 with VL1, and LED-VID-0015 (VL2 slot access, BSY, CPU waits) is `deferred`. `vdc_snapshot_mid_dma`, `vdc_dma_lenr_live` (review H-01 regression), `vdc_advance_pure` and `vdc_random_regs` (32 programs, gate and sanitizer presets).
