# Phase 4 Plan 18: seeded-prng-v3 and the restore refusals for the new regions Summary

**Additive power-on policy seeded-prng-v3 (VRAM, SATB and palette drawn from three new splitmix64 domains, literals from an independent script), the new default guest policy list zero, v3 seed 1, v3 seed 2, and 70 red-capable restore refusal rows with hostile-corpus flips for the seven Phase 4 regions; no second serial, epoch or text bump.**

## What was built

- **Task 1, commit 128ca83.** `DS_POLICY_SEEDED_V3 = 4`; three new `ds_domain` rows and constants `VRAM____` 0x5652414D5F5F5F5F, `SATB____` 0x534154425F5F5F5F and `PAL_____` 0x50414C5F5F5F5F5F (derived from the names by the script, not copied from a note); `ds_policy_of`, `ds_policy_is_seeded`,
  `ds_policy_name` and `ds_policy_version` (v3 returns 3). `ds_poweron_apply` runs the RAM draw for every seeded policy, every v2 draw for `policy >= V2` (test `>=`, Pitfall 13) and then `apply_v3` for `policy >= V3`: 65,536 bytes of VRAM, 512 of SATB and 1,024 of palette, then bits 1..7 of every
  odd palette byte cleared. `profile_fields.def`, `profkinds.c`, `dec_powr` (upper bound V3) change in this one commit, so policy 4 is accepted exactly when it exists. `snapshot_refusal_fields` moves its unknown-policy row to policy 5 (`policy-5`). `guestkinds.c` default list is zero, v3 seed 1, v3
  seed 2. `powkinds.c` grammar gains `vram@N=`, `satb@N=`, `pal@N=` and the structural items `video=zero`, `drawn=vram|satb|pal`, `palbits=clear` and `vid=constants` (every VDC, VCE, beam and DMA byte 0 except the three boot fields).
- **Independent literals.** A python3 script outside the repository (arbitrary-precision masks, no C and no DuoSpool run) first reproduced the committed v1 `ram@0` and `ram@8184` literals and the v2 cpu, mpr and tmr bytes for seed 42, then derived the v3 domains: vram first 16 bytes
  1e392bd630c8e4695e2a728c89e5f489 and last 8 29d8a7e87a2d484f; satb first 16 e6e33e2447afd9d6ed1f78c865bf9851 and last 8 6a9fc2f65e58c243; palette first 16 after the odd-byte clear 590155009c0192003c01cc013001c601 and last 8 1801810016006000. The method sentence is in each case comment. Mutants
  seen red: a changed VRAM constant failed both draw-order and independence cases, and a missing palette bit clear failed `poweron_v3_smoke` and every `snapshot_restore` boundary (the decoder refuses an odd palette byte above 1).
- **Task 2, commit a95170a.** Seventy rows in the `sk_rows` table of snapkinds.c, driven by `ED_U8` / `ED_U16` / `ED_U32` macros over hand-kept payload offsets (documented in a comment block): VCE0 (cta 0x200 / 0x1FF), PALT (odd byte 2, 1, 0xFF), VDC0 (AR 32 / 31, status 0x40 / 0x3F, R03 and R04
  nonzero, one bit outside the mask of each of R05 to R0F), BEAM (stage, v_state, v_togo 513 refused and 512 accepted, display_k 511 accepted and 512 / 0xFFFF refused inside VDW and a line refused outside it, rcr_counter, frame_lines, bg_y, mwr_active, line_dirty, sprite_pend, vd_off and rcr_off
  windows, vd_irq_phase, rcr_origin), the line_idx bound (time / 1365 + 3, 2^63, 2^64 - 1 refused with lat_div 2 so only that rule fires; the limit itself accepted), `lat-div-5`, `lat-div-1`, `lat-div-0-late` (line_idx 5, lat_div 0), the four 0-or-1 rules (burst and vd_pending in
  `snapshot_refusal_beam`, satb_pending and satb_done_valid in `snapshot_refusal_vdma`), VDMA (dma_state 3, dma_done 65,537 / 65,536, `dma-word-0` as a running transfer with zero master clocks per word, 9 refused, 8, 12 and 16 accepted). Every refusal is declared `field` and the harness compares the
  whole instance byte-for-byte and by member afterwards. A mutant of snapshot.c without the v_togo, line_idx, lat_div and dma_word rules turned `snapshot_refusal_beam`, `_timeline` and `_vdma` red; it was reverted. The hostile corpus (kind `snapshot_hostile`, 773 inputs now, 567 refused and 206
  accepted) flips every byte of VCE0, VDC0, BEAM and VDMA, adds four refused palette inputs (bit 1 of an odd byte) and sampled bit flips of PALT, VRAM (16 inputs, never all 65,536 bytes) and SATB. `snapshot_hostile.toml` line 5 stays exactly 400 characters; its "about 530" became "about 770" in
  place and the new prose is a new comment line. `field_flip.toml` and `profiles/pce-hucard-2btn/r1.toml` are untouched.
- **F-35 note (recorded as the plan asks).** The model-parameter cap of 4 is exhausted (`tma_zero`, `irq_i_sample`, `vd_irq_phase`, `rcr_origin`); Phase 5 cannot add a row without reopening D-113.
- **Sizes (unchanged by this plan, hand figures of 04-03 still equal the build).** `sizeof(struct ds_instance)` 1,141,096 bytes; snapshot 92,255 bytes (25,021 + 67,178 payload + 7 frame headers of 8 bytes). Region payloads: VCE0 3, PALT 1,024, VDC0 45, BEAM 33, VDMA 25, VRAM 65,536, SATB 512 (the
  `snapshot_hash` kind and `sk_stream_size()` check both against `ds_snapshot_size()` on every run).

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Four refusal cases instead of one.** The plan names `snapshot_refusal_vdc.toml` only, but the expect list of a case is capped at 511 characters (`PK_LIST_MAX 512`) and a TOML line at 400, and the plan's rule list needs about 70 rows. The rows are split over
  `snapshot_refusal_vdc` (VCE0, PALT, VDC0), `snapshot_refusal_beam`, `snapshot_refusal_timeline` and `snapshot_refusal_vdma`; all four are appended to REQUIRED. Labels named by the plan (`dma-word-0`, `lat-div-5`, `lat-div-0-late`) are used verbatim.
2. **[Rule 2 - Missing coverage] Files touched beyond `files_modified`.** `src/core/ds_core.h` (one comment naming policy 4), `scripts/check` (one comment naming the default list), `tests/conformance/cases/snapshot_restore_sampled.toml` (its request now binds seeded-prng-v3, which is the proof that
  a snapshot of policy 4 restores, a behaviour the plan lists without naming a case), and `poweron_zero`, `poweron_draw_order` and `poweron_v2_smoke` (`video=zero` / `vid=constants` items appended, so D-167's "zero, v1 and v2 give zero VRAM, SATB and palette" and the zero registers are asserted, not
  only implied). Strengthening only; no expectation was removed or loosened.
3. **[Note] `poweron_v3_draw_order` has `pins = []`.** LED-PROF-0002 already names `poweron_draw_order` as its pinning test and the inventory check enforces that back-link; the ledger was not in scope. The case comment says it extends the entry.
4. **[Note] Literal split.** The `expect` line of a case is capped at 400 characters, so the first 16 bytes of each video array and the v2 registers are in `poweron_v3_draw_order` and the last-word literals of each array plus the RAM and MPR literals are in `poweron_v3_domain_independence`.
5. **[Note] Two lines of snapkinds.c are 418 characters.** Both pre-date this plan (lines 621 and 1581 of the 04-03 tree) and are in a C file, which the line check of the case tooling does not scan; left alone.
