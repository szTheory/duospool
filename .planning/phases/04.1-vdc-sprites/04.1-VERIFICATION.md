**Detail 3 (collision gating, non-display lines).** `beam.c:237-238` stores collision in bit 0 and overflow in bit 1 of `sprite_pend` at EV_LINE. `beam.c:277-283` raises them at EV_RCR through `ds_vdc_flag`, which gates bit 0 on CR bit 0 and bit 1 on CR bit 1 (`vdc.c:263-278`).
The non-display clause holds by construction, which is why the offdisplay mutant is equivalent. Outside VDW, `display_k` is 0xFFFF (`beam.c:105`), so the line coordinate is 65599. No sprite reaches that coordinate, because Y is at most $3FF and the height at most 64.
The restore path keeps that invariant: `snapshot.c:699` refuses display_k != 0xFFFF outside VDW, and `snapshot.c:715` refuses sprite_pend != 0 outside a display line. The case is `snapshot_refusal_reach` with the rows `sprite-pend-vds` and `sprite-pend-vdw`.
