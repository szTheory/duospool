# Phase 4.1: Code Review Report (VDC sprites, core and gate)

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-12
**Range:** f9d9d69..HEAD (HEAD 22a55eb)
**Depth:** deep (SATB decode -> slot scan -> dot words -> composition -> sprite_pend -> EV_RCR -> status; snapshot rules; judge, sweeps, toy, classes, claims)

## Summary

The sprite core is sound as code: I found no out-of-bounds access, no undefined behaviour and no determinism or chunking defect.
The SATB index is masked to 256 words. The pattern address of the largest legal case (32 x 64, PC $7F0) ends at word $FFFF.
Dots are bounded by active_w <= 682. Every VRAM word goes through the bounds-checked accessor.
The STATE part runs at every EV_LINE with or without a sink, and sprite_pend is serialised in BEAM.

The defects are in the evidence and the claims built on the code:
- **H-01:** two MAN rows rest on readings that the project's own ledger text calls readings: the 12-slot count, and OR set with only 16 sprites on the line.
- **M-01:** the "no flag without the enable" converse is tagged MAN again. This is the Phase 4 gate review M-01 class, in a fixture written after that fix.
- **M-02:** two drawing rules are never exercised: Y-flip of a tall sprite, and dropping the right cell of a half-taken sprite. A mutant of each survives every sprite case and the golden.
- **M-03:** a GUEST group is killable only by F0, while a text claims a toy covers it. This is the Phase 4 H-01 claim class.
- **M-04:** no chunk, restore, observer or non-interference case runs a sprite fixture.
- **M-05, M-06:** the README sprite paragraph and the signed-off validation file state things the fixtures contradict.

Experiments (outside the repository; nothing in the repo was modified):
- The gate's ds_runner, run from a scratch copy of build/gate:
  - `--video-out` reproduced the spr_basic golden eaf53309... with shasum -a 256.
  - A small Python parser of the DSVR stream checked the per-frame register values, slots, covering and flags of both sprite streams.
- Three scratch cases ran the chunk_equiv, vid_noninterference and snapshot_restore_video kinds on spr-limit. All passed, including 26,022 restores.
- Two single-statement mutants of vid.c were built from a scratch `git archive` and run against every sprite case.
- shasum -a 256 of the built images matches the four changed manifests.

### L-01: OR and CR never reach the status on a line without EV_RCR (window end at or past 1365 clocks)

**Files:** `src/core/beam.c:184` (rcr_off none), `src/core/beam.c:238` (sprite_pend set), `src/core/beam.c:277-283` (raised only at EV_RCR), `ledger/entries/LED-VID-0012.toml` (statement)

**Issue:**
- With divider 4, HDS 2 and HDW 40 (HDR $0328), the RCR offset is (2+40+2)*8*4 = 1408, which is 1365 or more, so the line has no EV_RCR. The window is clipped to 317 dots.
- Eighteen covering sprites set OVERFLOW in the record, but sprite_pend is overwritten at the next EV_LINE, so the OR status bit (and CR) is never raised, frame after frame.
- LED-VID-0012 says "raised at the end of the active window of the displayed line" and D-160 is silent on this case. The loss is undocumented.

**Fix:** raise pending sprite flags at the next EV_LINE (before overwriting) when rcr_off is none, or at the clip point; otherwise record the loss in the LED-VID-0012 assumption and D-160 and pin it with one row.

### L-02: Restore accepts sprite_pend on a non-display line (unreachable state; Phase 4 core L-01 class)

## Checked and found sound (no finding)

- **Core bounds:**
  - satb_word masks to 256 words.
  - searched is at most 64 (2d + 1 <= 63 for d < 32).
  - covering is at most 64.
  - The pattern word is at most $FFFF for every CGX/CGY mask.
  - dx - 32 < aw <= 682 before any dot store.
  - y + h fits u32; plain-integer Y and X with no wrap, as D-159 decides.
- **Core semantics:**
  - Slot statement min(16, 2(e-2)) with e from HSR/HDR at EV_LINE.
  - Take, left-cell-only and drop with no early stop.
