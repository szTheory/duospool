# Phase 4.1 Plan 02: Sprite slots, overflow and collision Summary

**Sprites obey the documented per-line slot limit: 16 sprites on a line with e = 10 fit, 18 drop two and set OVERFLOW (MAN H7-19), 12 slots with e = 8, the cap of 16 where the formula gives 20, 2d + 1 entries searched on a narrow display, a 32-wide sprite with one slot left drawn as its left cell, and sprite 0 over another sprite sets COLLISION; the record
carries slots_used and both flags, the OR and CR status bits reach guest RAM through the status register, and the tenth toy shows the cap live.**

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] A new judge check, `spr_line`.** The plan's rows for `slots_used` and OVERFLOW need a header-field row; the grammar had none (ROWS judge index and value lists, STRUCT a closed list). `videokinds.c` (the check, its 8 selftests and the offsets of D-161 for bytes 33 and 34 and the flag bits 6 and 7) and `vexp-rules.awk` (`ck["spr_line"]`,
   not a grid check, the frame is the author's `held` mark) were added in Task 1.
2. **[Rule 3] Collision detection landed in Task 1.** The 18 sprite line cannot fit 256 dots without overlap, so sprite 0 is over sprite 1 there and every `spr_line` row carries COLLISION; the scan, the dot marks and the CR bit of `sprite_pend` therefore came with the slot scan. Task 2 judged them.
3. **[Note] The status is polled, not taken as IRQ1.** The plan says an IRQ1 handler reads the status into guest RAM; `spr_limit` reads the status register after each scene's clean frame (a read clears bits 0 to 5, H7-4) and `vdc_stat` already judges the level that follows the status (LED-IRQ-0009). The status bits reach guest RAM through the register; the
   delivery of OR and CR as IRQ1 is not asserted.
4. **[Rule 1 - Bug in the plan's toy design] The cap shows only at e >= 11.** `slots = min(16, 2(e - 2))` equals 16 at e = 10, so the toy "limit 16 to 17" changes nothing in the 256-wide setup. The 17 sprite scene runs with HDE 5 (e = 12, 20 by the formula) and carries check 6 and the `lim17` and `cap` rows; the 16 and 18 point rows stay at e = 10 as MAN.
   Without this `spr_limit` would not have turned red.
5. **[Rule 3] Cases and lines moved earlier.** The inventory rules (a literal scene needs its sweep, a toy or exemption line and a policy diff entry) put `video_fault_sweep_spr_limit`, the exemption line, `spr-limit@2` in `vdc_poweron_policy_diff` and the REQUIRED lines in Task 1, and the golden of spr_basic had to be repinned there (Task 1 fills `slots_used`).
   `mutate_sweep_spr_limit` joined REQUIRED in Task 2. The pin `pins = ["LED-VID-0012"]` of `video_spr_limit` waited for Task 3 (INV_PIN_BACKLINK).
6. **[Rule 3] Files outside `files_modified`:** `videokinds.c`, `vexp-rules.awk`, `ds_vdc.h`, `vdc.c` (comment), `spr_basic.vexp` (repin), `vdc_poweron_policy_diff.toml`, `mutate_sweep_vdc_stat.toml` (27 checks, deadline 6.2 million), `LED-VID-0011.toml` (the witness comment), `docs/ci-timings.md`, `fixtures/manifest/vdc_stat.toml`.
7. **[Note] Manifest repins.** `vdc_stat` old `8ff8484b...`, new `054ab276...`; `spr_limit` repinned with each of its three images (shasum -a 256, same commits). `vdc_stat` runs 5.55 million clocks to its verdict now (deadline 6.2 million, was 5 million).
