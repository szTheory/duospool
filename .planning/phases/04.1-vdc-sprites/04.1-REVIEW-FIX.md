**M-04 (deviation).** The ruling asked to extend the ROM lists of the existing cases, and to add no new cases. That cannot be done as written:
- `chunk_equiv` and `observer` take their ROM from the `fixture` key.
- `vid_noninterference` and `snapshot_restore_video` take one `rom=` item.
- Each case has one `until_mclk`. The sprite-flag stops of spr-limit start in frame 5 (about 1.84 million clocks), while the vce-pal restore case ends at 0.6 million.
- `chunk_equiv` also compares past the guest's done state (D-95), which for spr-limit is about 6.8 million clocks.

A ROM list would therefore mean changing four kind parsers and either raising the vce-pal deadlines (cost) or leaving the sprite run short (no coverage). Instead, one case per kind on spr-limit was added, with the kinds unchanged except one optional item:

| Case | Setup |
|------|-------|
| `chunk_equiv_video_spr_limit` | 1364, 1366, 3072 and vframe, to 7.5 million clocks |
| `vid_noninterference_spr_limit` | to 1.9 million clocks |
| `observer_spr_limit` | whole, chunk:7 and stop:5000, to 1.9 million clocks |
| `snapshot_restore_video_spr` | schedule 3072, to 1.9 million clocks, item `sprite-pend` |

The new item `sprite-pend` of `snapshot_restore_video` requires a restore at a stop where the source holds `sprite_pend != 0`, for every policy and schedule. The run had 1,236 restores, 12 of them with flags pending. The case is red with a 1.5 million clock deadline (one-off).
Measured user CPU: 1.11, 0.31, 1.34 and 1.07 s, about 3.8 s in all, recorded in `docs/ci-timings.md`. The 7-clock schedule was left out of the chunk case because `observer_spr_limit` runs chunk:7 on the same ROM, with the vid logs compared. This also closes security flag U-02.

**L-02 / U-01 (deviation on the stage).** `dec_beam` refuses `sprite_pend != 0` when `v_state != 1`. The suggested stage rule ("stage not 0") was not applied, because it would refuse a reachable state. On a display line whose window ends at or past 1365 clocks (the L-01 case), there is no EV_RCR.
That line holds its flags pending at stage 0 until the next EV_LINE overwrites them. A snapshot taken there must restore. Such a state raises nothing, because EV_LINE comes next, so the `v_state` rule alone closes the U-01 impact.
The rows `sprite-pend-vds` (refused) and `sprite-pend-vdw` (accepted) are defined next to `sprite-4` in snapkinds.c. They live in `snapshot_refusal_reach`, because adding them to `snapshot_refusal_beam` would push its expect line over 400 characters. With the rule disabled, `sprite-pend-vds` is accepted and the case is red (one-off).
