# Phase 4.1 Plan 01: Sprite decode and drawing Summary

**Sprites from the SATB are decoded and drawn: a 16 x 16 sprite copied in by the SATB DMA appears at its documented coordinate with its documented index, both widths, three heights, flips, palette bank, SPBG and entry-order priority and partial clipping are judged by 32 hand-derived rows in a solid and an asymmetric scene, the SATB equals its source block in three
power-on policies, and gate levers (2) and (3) of D-176 are applied with honest before and after figures.**

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Files outside `files_modified`.** The policy loops of the video runs live in `tests/conformance/vdckinds.c` and `runner.h` (not in guestkinds.c, videokinds.c, obskinds.c or snapkinds.c, which were listed), and the four video cases carry the policy text; `ds_vdc.h` gained the `ds_vid_spr` struct; `vdckinds.c` gained the second form of
   `vdc_satb_data` (the SATB source of spr_basic is entries, not an arithmetic progression, so the existing form could not express it); `vdc_poweron_policy_diff.toml` got `spr-basic@2` (named in the notes of 04-13).
2. **[Rule 1 - Bug] `bg_enab.s` read an uninitialised SATB.** With SB set in two phases and no SATB written, a seeded power-on drew random sprites: `vdc_poweron_policy_diff` failed for bg-enab (1,681 records at or after frame 2). The fixture now zeroes 256 VRAM words at word $1000 and arms the DVSSR copy before the first phase that sets SB, and the counted hold before
   write 6 is shortened from 57,200 to 53,072 cycles so the mid-frame write sits where it sat (machine time 5,056,413 before, 5,056,422 after, 9 clocks); manifest repinned (old 60501acb...,
   new 868bfc27..., shasum -a 256, same commit). No vexp row changed. `bg.inc` was not edited.
3. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Sweep, exemption and policy lines moved earlier.** The inventory rules of the review fix M-04 (VIDEO_SCENE_NO_SWEEP, VIDEO_SCENE_UNTOYED, VIDEO_FIXTURE_NO_POLICY) make a literal scene with no sweep, no toy or exemption line and no policy list entry a red gate, so Task 2 carries `video_fault_sweep_spr_basic`, the exemption line and the policy list
   entry; Task 3 carries a second sweep, `video_fault_sweep_spr_basic_asym` (one scene per sweep case, as `video_fault_sweep_bg_scroll_asym`), and Task 4 holds the golden, the witness and the pin.
4. **[Note] The tracer row of the plan has two distinct values.** The text asks for 0 at dots 38 and 39, 303 at 40 to 55 and the background again at 56 to 58 over an all transparent background: two distinct values, which the non-vacuity rule of D-171 refuses (at least 3). The scene keeps one opaque tile of pixel 15 at BAT row 2 column 7 (index 15 at dots 56 to 63), which
   is the third value, and the whole of BAT column 22 as a stripe for the rows of later groups.
5. **[Note] "Plane byte order" does not exist for sprites.** Each plane of a sprite pattern is its own word (SGk at +16k), so the page-stated plane significance is judged in the solid scene (pixels 12, 3, 10 and 5 over distinct planes) and the asymmetric scene judges the leftmost pixel and the left half in one word under ASSUMED LED-VID-0011.
6. **[Note] F2 is not declared by the solid groups.** The first indices of their rows (303, 15, 335, 447) have equal low bits, so F2 cannot act (the sweep said VIDEO_GROUP_VACUOUS); the asymmetric groups declare F0, F1, F2 and F7 and the exemption text says so.
7. **[Note] Lever values.** Lever (3) measured about 0.1 s and lever (2) 2.7 s: the stage stays over its 60 s trigger on a loaded machine (86 s before, 59 to 86 s after, depending on load) and the plan could not bring it down by any lever that weakens nothing. The efficiency review after the first CI run can restore the schedule 3072 at no real cost.
8. **[Note] Estimate.** The plan estimated 120,000 tokens; the realized diff is about 20,500 by chars/4 over the added lines (36 files, 846 insertions, 57 deletions).
