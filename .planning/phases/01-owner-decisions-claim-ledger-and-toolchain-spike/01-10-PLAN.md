<objective>
Run the pre-registered measurements for both skeletons and prove the results are complete. Bench wrappers call the option hooks, run-all.sh executes every register row with the protocol's exact commands, and scripts/check-results checks the results against the protocol register, with must-fail controls.

Purpose: TOOL-01 / ROADMAP SC1 requires measured clean and incremental build time, test time, lines of glue, tools required, the installed-consumer build, the fuzz run and portability notes for both options. This plan produces those numbers once, by the pre-registered method, before anyone scores them. It implements D-12 (measurements, including the D-12 cold/warm split), D-15 (not-demonstrated rows) and D-36 (stable-only timings).
Output: spikes/tool-01/bench/{clean,build,edit,restore,test,run-all}.sh, spikes/tool-01/results/ (raw JSON, results.tsv, gates outputs), scripts/check-results, tests/results/*, and the scripts/check `results-negatives` stage.
</objective>
