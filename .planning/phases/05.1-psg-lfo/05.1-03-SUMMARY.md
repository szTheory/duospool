## Findings

1. **Trigger (3) fires on the instruction measure** (+28.65 and +30.23 percent against the 05-01 baseline). It is recorded, not decided on. Phase 5.1 itself adds +5.44 and +4.14 percent, and about four fifths of the total was there at checkpoint A.

3. **The `dyn` group's word $08 rests on the P8-7 reading of DX-4.** P8-7 lists $08 as -8; P8-8 draws only 4 added bits. D-184 classes the addition MAN and no row wraps the 12-bit sum; the README and the ledger name the reading. A verifier may want to look at it.
4. **Three clauses are C-level only:** W26 (the R8 store), W30 (the TRG freeze) and C01 (the 12-bit period mask) survive at guest level by design and are killed by psg_decode, psg_fold and psg_lfo_trg, and psg_pure_function.
5. **LED-AUD-0002 is eligible for promotion** on its clause table and was not promoted (see Task 2).
