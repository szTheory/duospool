## Arithmetic re-derived (RESEARCH section 2; P8-6..P8-9, H8-23)

- F0' = (F0 + 4096 + (w << s) - (16 << s)) & 4095, with 0 read as 4096 and s = 2 x (CTL - 1).
  - F0 512, w $18 (24): s 0 gives 520; s 2 gives 512 + 96 - 64 = 544; s 4 gives 512 + 384 - 256 = 640.
  - F0 512, w $08: 504.
  - F0 16, w 0, s 0: 4096 & 4095 = 0, read as 4096 (ASSUMED, lfo_wrap).
- Samples: at d = 6 a cycle is 192 x F' master clocks, which is F' samples. Two cycles are 1,024, 1,040, 1,088 and 1,280 samples for CTL 0..3.
- Toy (s = CTL - 1): CTL 1 gives s 0 (520, green); CTL 2 gives s 1 (528, distance 1,056, red); CTL 3 gives s 2 (544, distance 1,088, red).
- lfo_dynamic:
  - F0 192 gives F0' 200 for w $18 and 184 for w $08.
  - The select-1 step is 6 x 128 x 150 = 115,200 clocks: 600 samples, exactly 3 cycles of 200.
  - A half table is 16 x 115,200 = 1,843,200 clocks = 9,600 samples: 48 cycles of 200 or 52.2 of 184.
  - The window is 4,032,000 clocks = 21,000 samples = 35 select-1 steps. That gives two switches, at 9,600 and 19,200 samples after a release whose first step comes one step later. Any restart phase still gives both values and one interior run of 16 steps, at least 50 cycles, which is more than 40.
  - A straddling cycle measures 184 + k/2 samples, where k is the number of its steps at 200. So there is at most one per switch, and it may round to a run value.
  - FLF 0: 6 x 128 x 256 = 196,608.
- lfo_static window:
  - The first select-0 reload after an R9 write comes at most 6 x 640 = 3,840 clocks later. from = 192 x F' + 2,000 is past that, so every counted step is at the new period.
  - The window spans 4 cycles minus 2,000 clocks, more than 3 cycles, so it holds at least three crossings.
  - The phase lasts at least 5.2 cycles + 2,000 clocks, past to = 5 cycles.
- Sum range: minimum 0 + 4096 + 0 - 256 = 3,840, maximum 4095 + 4096 + 496 - 256 = 8,431. The sum is unsigned and never wraps; the shift is formed only while CTL != 0.
- Fold bound: the span is at most 600 clocks (ds_max_step_mclk, pinned by a psg_fold row). At d = 3 with F1 = FLF = 1 and F0' = 1, select 1 and select 0 each have an event every 3 clocks. That is at most 400 events at 200 instants, under the 600-iteration bound (RESEARCH: about 420 with noise and samples).
  Offsets stay at or below 600 + 6,291,456 = 6,292,056 < 2^23 = 8,388,608 (static assert, psg.c line 20).
- Restore bounds (C-09):
  - d x 4096 x 256 is 6,291,456 at d = 6 and 3,145,728 at d = 3.
  - At d = 3 and time 0 the grid is r = 1 mod 3. Both 12,289 and 3,145,729 lie on it, and 3,145,729 is above 3,145,728.
- Decode rows:
  - R9 $81, $82, $83 & $83 post $0081, $0082, $0083; R8 150 = $96 posts $0096.
  - Select 1 R3 $08 then R2 $83 posts $0883. Mutant K04 (R3 mask $07) would post $0083; K03 (R2 mask $FC) would post $0880.
