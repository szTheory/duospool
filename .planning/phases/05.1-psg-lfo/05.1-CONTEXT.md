- **Fold bound:** D-221: a fold spans at most `ds_max_step_mclk()` = 600 master clocks; the coupled loop is bounded by that span (at most 600 iterations per fold; about 400 events at d = 3, F1 = FLF = 1); offsets stay below 600 + 6,291,456 < 2^23; arithmetic on offsets from the fold point only.
- **Errata that bind 05.1-01:** D-219 (modulation while CTL != 0 and select 0 and 1 on; TRG 1 holds wave[1][0] - 16 and freezes the select-1 counter); D-220 (restart edges beyond key-on, including the TRG 1 -> 0 release of an uncoupled select 1, are named constants with LED-AUD-0004 or -0011 and carry no MAN row); D-221; D-229 (stop rule about 80 percent);

- **C-09 Select-1 restore bound (Phase 5 review fix WR-02, after 05-10).** The Phase 5 restore decoder (snapshot.c dec_psgt) bounds every running channel distance, select 1 included, by d x 4096 (24,576 at d = 6, 12,288 at d = 3) and both noise distances by d x 64 x 31: the reachable bounds of
  the restoring d. The coupled bound 6,291,456 of D-203 is LFO behaviour and reads as 05.1-01's (D-234). So 05.1-01 Task 1, with the coupled fold, restores the coupled bound for a running select 1 in dec_psgt: d x 4096 x 256 (6,291,456 at d = 6, 3,145,728 at d = 3) whatever the current
  coupling, since a pending coupled distance outlives a coupling change until its next reload. The row p-rem1coupled of snapshot_refusal_psgt turns from field to accept (add a refused row at the next on-grid value), and the d = 3 select-1 row 12,289 of psg_restore_divisor turns to
  accept (add 3,145,729 refused). snapshot.c and those two cases lie outside the plan's files_modified and are authorised here. Task 3's restore stop between two coupled select-1 steps depends on this change. No serial version or epoch bump: the layout is unchanged, the decoder
  accepts a superset of what Phase 5 accepts, and every Phase 5 snapshot stays restorable (the 04.1 L-02 precedent: a restore rule is no format change).
